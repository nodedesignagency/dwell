# Screens and states

Everything the brief asks the app to show, so nothing is missed. Each design the user sends is mapped here: some rows are designed directly, the rest are derived from those designs' system. When the designs are done, every row still ⬜ in **Design** is a gap to raise with the user.

**Design:** ⬜ not designed yet · 🎨 designed (name the design) · 🔁 derived from another design (name it)
**Built:** ⬜ not started · 🟡 in progress · ✅ done (all states, light and dark, large text, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, dial variants)

Built 🟡 on a page we finished means the code is complete for every state but hasn't yet compiled in Xcode: cloud sessions can't build SwiftUI. It turns ✅ once the page builds and its previews are checked on a Mac.

Designs so far: **Today** (Figma `WpF9jJZdOG3ox1bR7iMT6o`, node 69:81); Today **"Getting Started"** (Figma, 12 Nov); Today **"After few minutes of getting started"** (screenshot, 12 Nov); Today **"Weekly Wrap"** (screenshot and Figma values, 12 Nov); **Your places** (screenshot, 12 Nov); **You** (screenshot, 12 Nov); **Time** (three screenshots, 12 Nov: the whole page, the part above the fold, and "All categories" at 4×); the **Weekly wrap** flow (screenshot of all eight frames, 13 Nov: the invite, then seven pages) and its pages **"Weekly Wrap 1"** ("This week.", screenshot, 13 Nov), **"Weekly Wrap 2"** ("Work vs yours", screenshot, the designer's comment and the stripes' reference image, 13 Nov), **"Weekly Wrap 3"** ("Weekends were all yours.", screenshot, the pattern's reference image and its placement in Figma, 13 Nov), **"Weekly Wrap 4"** ("Where you spent the most time", screenshot, the pattern's reference image and its Figma placement, 14 Nov), **"Weekly Wrap 5"** ("Earliest exit this week", screenshot, Figma colours, the clock face and the pattern's SVG, 13 Nov), **"Weekly Wrap 6"** ("Explorer week", screenshot, the pattern's reference image and its Figma placement, 14 Nov) and **"Weekly Wrap 7"** ("Postcard from your week", screenshot, the stripes' reference image and the stamp's SVG, 13 Nov); **Insights** (screenshot, 13 Nov: one insight opened from Today's deck); the **Onboarding flow** (an overview of every frame, 13 Nov: first run, the returning user, and the moments after the first day) and its first four pages, **My world**, **Your places**, **See where it all goes** and **Start my day** (screenshots, 13 Nov); then account setup, **Save your world**, **When's your birthday?**, **One last thing**, **Account setup stopped** and **Not in your region yet** (screenshots, 14 Nov); **Set your tone** and **Is this home?**, sheets over Today (screenshots, 14 Nov). **Share this insight** (screenshot, 14 Nov).
